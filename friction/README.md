The root-level `FRICTION.md` is the friction log (currently empty). Record friction here, in `friction/`, or in `FRICTION.md` as development proceeds. Examples of things worth logging:

- Setup steps that failed or were undocumented (Goose toolchain, `.dockerignore`, pnpm workspaces).
- CLI or protocol behavior that surprised a new user.
- Milestones that failed on purpose (`NOT_BUILT` targets in the Makefile) and why.
- Open change requests needing human sign-off (`spec/CHANGE_REQUESTS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`).
